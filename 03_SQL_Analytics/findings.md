# SQL Analytics — Key Findings

- Olist generated **$15.4M revenue** from 96,477 delivered orders
- **São Paulo drives ~40% of revenue** — the single most important market
- Orders and unique customers are nearly equal — almost **zero repeat purchases**
- Delivery delays and 1-star reviews are directly correlated
- **Credit card + installments** dominate payments — customers are price-sensitive
- Top product categories follow a high-ticket low-frequency buying pattern
