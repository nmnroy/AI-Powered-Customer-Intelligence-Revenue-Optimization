# AI-Powered Customer Intelligence & Revenue Optimization Platform

## 1. Business Problem
E-commerce businesses generate a huge amount of customer and sales data every day. However, without proper analysis, this data provides very little value for business growth. Companies often struggle to identify their most valuable customers, understand purchasing behavior, reduce customer churn, and make informed business decisions.

The goal of this project is to convert raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights using data analytics and machine learning. By analyzing customer behavior, estimating Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), identifying churn risks, and segmenting customers, this project helps businesses improve customer retention, optimize marketing strategies, and increase overall revenue.


## 2. Business Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior to understand buying patterns.
* Identify high-value customers using RFM and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) analysis.
* Detect customers who are at risk of churning.
* Discover sales and product trends that influence business performance.
* Build interactive dashboards to support data-driven business decisions.
* Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention and increase revenue.

## 3. Business KPIs

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
* Customer Retention Rate
* Customer Churn Rate
* Monthly Revenue Growth
* Repeat Purchase Rate
* Average Customer Rating

## 4. Business Questions

1. Which customers generate the highest revenue?
2. Which customer segments contribute the most to overall sales?
3. Which products and categories perform the best?
4. What are the monthly sales and revenue trends?
5. Which customers are likely to churn?
6. What is the Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) of different customer segments?
7. How effective is customer retention over time?
8. What business strategies can help increase revenue and customer retention?

## 5. Project Scope

This project covers the complete customer analytics lifecycle, from raw data processing to business recommendations. It includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL analytics, Excel analysis, customer segmentation (RFM), Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) analysis, Cohort Analysis, churn prediction, interactive Power BI dashboards, and business recommendations to improve customer retention and revenue growth.

## 6. Expected Business Outcome

By the end of this project, the business will gain a clear understanding of customer behavior, identify its most valuable customers, detect customers at risk of churn, and uncover key sales trends. The insights and dashboards developed in this project will support data-driven decision-making, improve customer retention strategies, optimize marketing efforts, and contribute to long-term revenue growth.
